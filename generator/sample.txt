Chapter 1

The old lighthouse had stood on the cliff for two hundred years, and in all that time no one had ever climbed to the very top. Mara pushed open the rusted door. It groaned like a living thing.

"Are you sure about this?" whispered Tom. "Nobody comes here anymore."

"That's exactly why we're coming," she said, and she began to climb.

Chapter 2

At the top, the wind howled through the broken glass. Something glimmered in the corner, and Mara's heart began to race.

"Look!" she cried. "It's a map!"
